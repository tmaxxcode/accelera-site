# Product Page Brief: Accelera Landing Page

## 1. Product & offer
- **What it is:** High-converting landing page for Accelera — Twin Cities home-service websites + booking + after-hours phone
- **Price:** Site $1,497 (fixed, 50% deposit / 50% on approval, two-week delivery) → Booking $597/mo optional → Phone $997/mo optional. Both monthly have 30-day money-back.
- **What's included:** 5-page WordPress site built to rank for [trade] + [city], custom no-account booking calendar, live after-hours phone with job capture to calendar, call tracking, you approve before launch
- **Guarantee/returns:** 30-day money-back on monthly components (booking & phone); approve live staging site before paying balance; WordPress on YOUR hosting — portable anytime
- **Shipping:** Digital delivery in 14 days
- **Primary transformation/benefit:** Stop losing jobs to missed calls and contact forms that don't book. Wake up to booked jobs instead of voicemails.

## 2. Buyer
- **Who they are:** Owner-operators of plumbing, roofing, auto repair, contracting businesses (1-10 trucks, $500K-$5M revenue) in 7-county Twin Cities metro
- **What they already tried:** SEO agencies ($2,500/mo retainers, zero calls), generic web designers, KUKUI/DealerSocket (auto), answering services that just take messages, DIY website builders
- **The objection that stops the sale:** Four core objections (from PAIN-BANK.md + MESSAGE-MAP.md):
  1. "Burned by SEO agencies" — Paid monthly retainers with vague reports, no calls
  2. "$997/mo is steep" — Wife does it for $0, answering service is $180
  3. "My wife takes the calls" — She knows customers, scripts can't judge job worth
  4. "Too busy" — On roof 10 hrs/day, no time for 45-min discovery calls or learning dashboards

## 6. Design direction (from DESIGN-DIRECTION.md — see full document at ~/AlCapital/landing/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md)
- **Style:** High-trust, professional, conversion-first. Not "agency cool" — owner-operator credible
- **Mood:** Competent, local, transparent. Twin Cities authority.
- **Reference benchmarks:** Unbounce services LP median 6.1% (target >6.1%); grade 5-7 copy converts 11.1% vs 5.3%
- **Hero layout intent:** Light hero only (Three.js loads AFTER content/CTA), message-matched (5 variants A-E), ONE CTA + click-to-call, no navigation (1:1 attention ratio)
- **Color:** Section-by-section mapping using all 9 palette tokens (primary #2B67BD, accent #00A859, bg #FFFFFF, fg #1A1A2E, card #F8FAFC)
- **Typography:** Inter system, display 700/-0.02em, body 500, responsive clamp scale (3.5rem→2.25rem headline)
- **Key interactive elements:** 
  - Missed-call calculator with labeled defaults per trade (Roofing $8K/120/28.1%, Plumbing $450/200/27%, Auto $650/180/54%, Contracting $3.5K/80/30%)
  - Booking demo: fully interactive 4-step flow, labeled "DEMO", no submission
  - Sticky mobile CTA bar (≤768px) with guarantee row
- **Motion:** Content/CTA render first, hero loads after, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, minimal entrance reveals only (count-up stats, slide transitions)
- **Responsive:** Mobile-first, 4 breakpoints (1440/1024/768/390), vertical stacking on mobile, horizontal on ≥1024px
- **Trust signals:** Sourced stats with inline citations, MN license badge (roofers), Tyler photo + direct number, "Twin Cities Only" badge, guarantee badges in Accent green
- **Offer stack:** 3 equal cards, no pre-selection, guarantee badges in #00A859
- **Full spec:** See DESIGN-DIRECTION.md for section-by-section visual treatment, motion table, responsive rules, trust signal implementation, build integration notes, and decision log
