### What They Sell

**Accelera** is a specialized service provider building websites, online booking, and after-hours phone answering for Twin Cities home-service companies. They build 5-page trade+city SEO websites (Home, Services, About, Reviews, Contact), put frictionless booking calendars on them (no account, no app, text confirmation), and answer phones after hours with live agents who capture job details and put them on the calendar.

**Core offer (3 tiers, each optional):**
1. **Website Build** — $1,497 fixed (50% deposit, 50% on approval). 2-week delivery. Client approves before launch.
2. **Monthly Booking SaaS** — $597/month. Calendar, SMS confirmations, no-account booking flow.
3. **Monthly Phone Answering** — $997/month. After-hours live answer, job capture, calendar sync. 42% of plumbing calls go to voicemail (99 Calls data).

**Tech stack (client implementation):** WordPress + Elementor for sites; custom booking calendar; after-hours phone via Telnyx/Twilio + LiveKit or Pipecat. Hosting on Vercel/Netlify/Cloudflare Pages free tier.

**Target geography:** Twin Cities 7-county metro only (Hennepin, Ramsey, Anoka, Dakota, Washington, Carver, Scott). 24 priority cities identified (pop >20k + 3 ICP locations).

**Trades served (ICP):** Plumbing, Auto Repair, Roofing, Contracting (general/specialty). Size: 1-10 trucks, $500K–$5M revenue.

### Features and Differentiators That Matter Commercially

1. **Trade+city SEO architecture** — Every title tag includes trade + city (e.g., "Minneapolis Emergency Plumber | Accelera Site"). Not generic "Minnesota" pages.
2. **Frictionless booking** — Customer picks service → picks time → enters address → gets text confirmation. No account, no app, no password.
3. **After-hours live phone** — Real agents answer, take job, read address back, put on calendar. Catches the 42% of calls that go to voicemail.
4. **Fixed price, no monthly SEO retainer** — $1,497 one-time. Competitors charge $700–$2,100/mo ongoing.
5. **Approve before launch** — Client sees full site, requests changes, then approves. Risk reversal.
6. **30-day money-back on monthly parts** — Booking and phone can be cancelled in first 30 days.
7. **Twin Cities only** — Not a national agency. Tyler is local. Face + direct number on site.
8. **No dashboard/setup burden** — Done-for-you vs. SaaS tools the owner has to manage.
9. **Original local data** — 44 contractors with no website, 87.5% booking gap from audit. Competitors don't have this data.
10. **Unified offer** — Site + booking + phone in ONE package. Competitors do one or two.

### Objections and Friction Points

**From discovery calls, plan research, and prospect conversations:**

1. **"I've been burned by SEO agencies"** — Previous agency took money, delivered nothing measurable. Trust is the #1 barrier.
2. **"$997/mo for phone is steep"** — Owner does the math: "That's $12K/year." Needs missed-call revenue math to justify.
3. **"My wife takes calls / I answer my own phone"** — Spouse/office manager fears complexity, losing control, or "another system to manage."
4. **"I'm too busy for a website project"** — Owner-operator on job sites 10+ hrs/day. 2-week delivery + approve-before-launch addresses this.
5. **"Do I own the site?"** — Ownership terms not yet confirmed (Waiting on Tyler). Needs clear answer.
6. **"What if the booking calendar breaks?"** — Reliability concern. No dashboard/setup burden message helps.
7. **"Why not just use Google My Business booking?"** — GMB booking is limited, no customization, no SMS confirmations.
8. **"Contractors don't search for websites"** — They search for "emergency plumber Minneapolis" — the site ranks for THEIR customers' queries, not "plumber website."
