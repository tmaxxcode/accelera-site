## AI Search Optimization Plan

**Target:** AI Overviews (Google), Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Claude citations

### Technical Implementation
1. **FAQPage schema** on every city×trade page — 5-10 Qs per page (e.g., "How much does a plumber website cost in Minneapolis?", "Do plumbers need online booking?", "What is after-hours answering for roofers?")
2. **Speakable markup** on key sections (pricing, guarantee, booking demo)
3. **Concise answer blocks** — 40-60 word direct answers to common questions, placed early in content
4. **Entity clarity** — Organization schema with sameAs (LinkedIn, Facebook, GBP, etc.)
5. **Service schema** — Service type: Web Design, Booking SaaS, Phone Answering; areaServed: Twin Cities metro cities

### Content for AI Citations
