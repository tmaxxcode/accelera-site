## Copy Rules (enforced by humanizer + reading-grade check)

1. **Grade 5–7 for landing pages** (Unbounce: 11.1% vs 5.3% conversion)
2. **Grade 6–8 for website** (mobile-first, owners read in truck)
3. **One idea per sentence** — max 20 words
4. **No em-dashes, no parentheticals, no emoji** (brand neutral)
5. **Active voice only** — "We build" not "Your site will be built"
6. **Specific numbers, not modifiers** — "28.1%" not "nearly 30%"
7. **Owner words** — "book a job" not "schedule an appointment"
8. **CTA next to guarantee** — "30-day money-back" adjacent to every primary button
