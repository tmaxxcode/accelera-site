## Architecture Flags

1. **New page type required: City Hub Page** — Neither Minneapolis nor St. Paul hub pages exist. Need new URL pattern: `/minneapolis`, `/st-paul` (and potentially `/minneapolis/plumber-website-design` for city×trade). These are marketing site pages, not blog posts.

2. **New page type required: Vertical Landing Page (city×trade)** — Pattern: `/minneapolis/plumber-website-design`, `/st-paul/roofing-website-design`. These are commercial landing pages on the marketing site, not blog. Requires: unique audit data per city/trade, embedded booking demo, missed-call calculator.

3. **Pillar + cluster structure:** Each city hub (pillar) links to 4 city×trade pages (cluster). Minneapolis hub → plumber, roofing, auto, contractor. St. Paul hub → same 4.

4. **Marketing site vs. blog:** All 11 shaped topics belong on the **marketing site** (Accelera's own site), not a blog. They are commercial landing pages with CTAs: "See Your Site Demo", "Calculate Missed Revenue", "Book Discovery Call".

5. **Cluster sequencing dependency:** The 4 Minneapolis city×trade pages should be built as a batch (shared internal linking, consistent structure, compound topical authority). Same for St. Paul batch.

6. **State-level pages separate:** Minnesota-level pages (`/minnesota/plumber-website-design`) are distinct from city×trade. Link from city hubs but don't cannibalize.

### Internal Linking Strategy
- Each city hub links to its 4 city×trade pages
- Each city×trade page links to: After-Hours page (if trade=plumbing/roofing), Booking page (all trades), Missed-Call Calculator
- After-Hours pages link back to relevant city×trade pages
- Booking page links to all city×trade pages
- State-level pages link to all relevant city×trade pages
- Homepage links to both city hubs

### CTA Strategy (All Pages)
- **Primary CTA:** "See Your [Trade] Site Demo" → Cal.com booking for discovery call
- **Secondary CTA:** "Calculate Your Missed Revenue" → Missed-call calculator tool
- **Tertiary CTA:** "Add Booking for $597/mo" / "Add After-Hours Phone for $997/mo" → Upsell paths

### Content Requirements Per Page

- Trade-specific messaging from MESSAGE-MAP.md
- Embedded interactive element (calculator, booking demo, or comparison table)
