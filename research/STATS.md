## Stats to AVOID (weak sourcing, widely repeated vendor claims)

| Stat | Why Avoid | Source Type |
|---|---|---|
| "62% of calls go unanswered" | Widely repeated by multiple vendors (CallRail, Invoca, others) without consistent primary study; sample and methodology vary | Vendor marketing claims |
| "$126K lost per year per business from missed calls" | Extrapolated from average ticket × call volume × answer rate; no single study with this exact figure | Vendor calculator outputs |
| "88% of consumers trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations" | BrightLocal 2016 figure; outdated and methodology changed | Old survey data |
| "50% of mobile searchers visit a store within a day" | Google 2014 "Micro-Moments" study; outdated for 2026 | Outdated study |

## Summary: Ready-to-Use Verified Stats

**For landing page proof bar (highest confidence):**
1. **Invoca:** 27% of home-service calls unanswered; <3% leave voicemail; 46% phone lead conversion (60M+ calls)
2. **99 Calls:** Roofers answer only 28.1% of calls live; plumbers 58% (36,498 contractor calls)
3. **Unbounce:** 5th–7th grade copy converts 11.1% vs 5.3% (41,000 pages); median services LP converts 6.1%
4. **BrightLocal 2025:** 97% read reviews; 54% visit website after positive reviews; 72% look at ≤3 businesses; 75% decide in <30 min (n=1,200+)

**For website trust stack:**
- Accelera internal: 44 top-rated Twin Cities contractors with no website; 7/8 audited prospects advertise booking but have no scheduler

**Mark as UNVERIFIED until traced:** ZoomInfo/Unbounce word count claim, Portent load speed claim, any stat without primary URL + date + sample size.
