## Executive Summary

**November in Minnesota is a HIGH-URGENCY transition month** — the pivot from fall maintenance to winter emergency season. Three factors converge:

1. **Heating season fully active** — furnaces/boilers running daily, first hard freezes
2. **First measurable snowfall** — typically Nov 10–20 in Twin Cities
3. **Pre-holiday deadline** — homeowners want systems reliable before Thanksgiving/Christmas

**Result:** Emergency call volume spikes 3–5× baseline for heating, roofing (ice dams), and plumbing (frozen pipes). Booking-based businesses capture this demand; phone-only businesses miss it.
