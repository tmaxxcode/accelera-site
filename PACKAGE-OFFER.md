## Sales Battle Cards (from competitor mining)

### Plumbing — Key Weaknesses to Emphasize
- "No online booking calendar" — customer must call or fill form (friction)
- "Account required for booking" — extra friction, lower conversion
- "AI voice, not live human" — negative positioning vs. Accelera's live answer
- "Custom quoting, no transparent pricing" — Accelera's $1,497 is transparent
- "No money-back guarantee" — Accelera offers 30-day money-back

### Roofing — Key Weaknesses to Emphasize
- Same patterns as plumbing, plus:
- Seasonal booking gaps (winter slowdown not addressed)
- Insurance claim paperwork not integrated into booking

### Auto Repair — Key Weaknesses to Emphasize
- "No scheduling flexibility" — customers want evenings/weekends
- "Contact form only" — low conversion vs. text/calendar
- "No after-hours coverage" — emergency calls go to voicemail
